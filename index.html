Our mission

Turning digital access into lasting opportunity.

Technology is more than a device. It is a way to learn, connect, create, and participate in a rapidly changing world.

Better Tomorrow Foundation is building a practical bridge across the digital divide by pairing equipment with training and local support.

Refurbished laptops, tablets, projectors, and learning tools for communities cut off from digital opportunity.

Partnerships and local guidance that help learners keep using their skills long after training ends.

Training curriculum

Four weeks to digital confidence.

A clear, practical introduction designed for first-time and early-stage computer users in low-connectivity settings.

Discuss a training partnership

Computer foundations

Basic parts, safe startup and shutdown, mouse and keyboard use, and introductory typing practice.

Essential productivity skills

Creating and saving files, organizing folders, and working with a word processor.

Internet & online safety

How the internet works, safer searching, privacy fundamentals, and sending a first email.

Practical digital projects

Simple presentations, spreadsheet basics, a collaborative project, and completion recognition.

Our story

A mission rooted in lived experience.

Founder Adam H. Harane’s vision was shaped by his experience among Sudanese families displaced to eastern Chad since 1999. He saw how limited access to technology could deepen barriers to education and opportunity.

Better Tomorrow Foundation grew from a simple conviction: when communities have the right tools, practical instruction, and trusted support, technology can become a bridge rather than another barrier.

“Technology should lift everyone.”

Adam H. Harane

Founder, Better Tomorrow Foundation
