The Story Behind the Mission

Our History

A vision shaped by displacement, resilience, and a belief in opportunity.

Our story

A mission rooted in lived experience.

Founder Adam H. Harane’s vision was shaped by his experience among Sudanese families displaced to eastern Chad since 1999. He saw how limited access to technology could deepen barriers to education and opportunity.

Better Tomorrow Foundation grew from a simple conviction: when communities have the right tools, practical instruction, and trusted support, technology can become a bridge rather than another barrier.

“Technology should lift everyone.”

Adam H. Harane

Founder, Better Tomorrow Foundation

Questions before getting involved?

Ask another question →
Where is the first pilot planned? +

The first pilot is focused on refugee and host-community learners in Farchana, eastern Chad. The Foundation is based in Roanoke, Virginia.

Can I donate a used computer or tablet? +

Refurbished devices are part of our plan. Please email us with the device type and condition before sending anything. Remove your personal data before donating.

Can I help remotely? +

We welcome conversations about remote support, including training materials, technical help, translation, outreach, and partnerships. Contact us to discuss current needs.

Can I donate money through this website? +

There is no online payment checkout configured yet. Contact the Foundation directly to discuss support, and confirm any payment instructions independently before sending funds.

Are financial contributions tax-deductible? +

Tax-deductibility depends on the organization's current legal status and applicable rules. Please ask for documentation before assuming your contribution qualifies.
